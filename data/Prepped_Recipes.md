### Acqua Pazza Chicken

**Price:** $9.80–$10.40  •  **Options:** Cut: Chicken Thighs / Chicken Breast

**Effort Level:** 2 Hats | **Equipment:** Pan | **Cooking Time:** 10-12 minutes | **Spice Level:** No Spice (0 chilli) | **Calories:** 409kcal

Acqua pazza or crazy water is one of our favourite sauces. Usually served with lightly poached fresh fish, this rich and savory sauce is remarkably simple to make, yet also extremely complex. The use of fresh summer veggies help create a strong flavor base (much like a mirepoix) from which we then layer on the richness of ripe cherry tomatoes and inject a shot of umami from the capers and artichoke hearts. Pair that with some shallow pan-fried chicken thighs and you're gonna want to acqua pazza everything for the next week.

**Ingredients**

```
Ingredients
Chicken Thighs: 200g
Zucchini: 40g
Onions: 30g
Red Peppers: 30g
Cherry Tomatoes: 30g
Artichoke hearts: 20g
Sundried tomatoes: 10g
Olives: 10g
Garlic: 5g

Sauce/Marinade: 140ml
Water, Cashew butter, Cornstarch, Coarse sea salt, Smoked paprika
```

**Allergy info:** Contains tree nuts.

**Effort Level:** 2 Hat | **Equipment:** Pot | **Cooking Time:** 10-12 minutes | **Spice Level:** Medium Spice (2 chilli) | **Calories:** 430kcal

### Bittergourd in Black Bean Sauce

**Price:** $8.90–$16.70  •  **Options:** Size: Single Portion / Double Portion | Meat: Chicken / Pork

**Effort Level:** 2 Hats | **Equipment:** Pan | **Cooking Time:** 5-6 minutes | **Spice Level:** No Spice (0 chilli) | **Calories:** 365kcal

We tend to use tartness and acid to help balance out the umami notes of a dish, but have you tried using the oft-maligned taste, bitter, instead?
Bitter gourd is one of our favorite ways to do this! The gourd starts off by acting as a sponge, soaking up the deep savory black bean sauce. Its natural bitter-sweet profile (think of a really dark chocolate or mole) then helps to cut through the initial savory-ness of the sauce, while each subsequent bites releases more sauce creating a time-release capsule of umami goodness. Toss in few more textures in the forms of carrots and mince and get ready to discover your new favorite dish.

**Ingredients**

```
Bittergourd: 120g
Pork Mince: 80g
Carrot: 30g
Garlic: 5g
Ginger: 5g

Marinade/Sauce: 65ml
Water, Black bean Paste, Shaoxing Wine, Sugar, Dark Soy Sauce, Cornstarch
```

May contain traces of tree nuts.
